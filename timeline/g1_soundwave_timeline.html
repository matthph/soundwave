MC 1.B

  • Interior Takara stamp

TF 1.A

  • Internal Takara stamp from MC 1.B
  • CassetteMan removed from tape door
  • Clean battery cover
  • Solid fists

TF 1.B

  • Takara stamp on battery cover
  • Mold scar in battery compartment
  • The mold used for Soundbreaker (black Soundblaster prototype)

TF 1.C

  • Hasbro and Takara Internal Stamp for international TF sales
  • 1.B Takara stamp on battery cover blocked
  • Solid fists

TF 2.D

  • New mold worked concurrently with 1.B/1.C
  • Slotted/hollow wrists
  • Takara internal stamp
  • No Hasbro stamp
  • Mold holes filled

TF 2.E

  • Internal Hasbro stamp re-added for Transformers international distribution
  • The base mold for International Soundwave figures - Japan, French Ceji, Mexican IGA
  • Slotted/hollow wrists

TF 3.F - Oct 1986

  • External buttons and hinge retooling
  • Hasbro/Takara co-branding maintained
  • Mold not used outside of Japan until 2004 TFC

TF 3.G Soundblaster - Jul 1987

  • Retooled chest to hold two cassettes
  • Black color scheme
