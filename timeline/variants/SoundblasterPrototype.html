G1 Soundwave Mold Evolution

Soundblaster Prototype

This figure is a Takara Engineering prototype made of black plastic instead of blue. It uses the TF1.B Soundwave mold, with identical stamping, fists, and mold marks. The theory is that this an aborted black Soundwave character. Takara was known to create new characters from existing molds, often in a black theme. The figure was cancelled due to Takara’s dependence on Hasbro’s Transformers cartoon ontinuity in 1985/1986, required to promote the toy. Since Mold TF1.C is a modified Mold TF1.B, the test shot would have had to have been created prior to 1985.

After the Japanese cartoon diverged from US Continuity in 1987, Takara regained creative independence and introduced Soundblaster into the Headmasters cartoon as a rebuilt Soundwave.

  • Uses the TF 1.B mold
